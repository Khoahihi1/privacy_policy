Privacy Policy

Khoahihi Tools

Version 4.0.0  ·  Effective: February 28, 2026

Our commitment: Khoahihi Tools does not sell, share, or exploit your personal data in any way. We collect only what is strictly necessary to provide the service.

1. Who We Are

Khoahihi Tools is a Chrome browser extension developed by Khoa, a first-year Electrical Engineering student at Ton Duc Thang University (TDTU), Vietnam. The extension provides:

  • AutoForm – AI-powered auto-fill for Google Forms on any website.
  • Elearning – Quiz and listening assistance for the TDTU eLearning platform.

2. Information We Collect

Data Purpose Storage
Email address Account login via Firebase Auth Firebase (Google Cloud)
Display name Personalize the UI Firestore + Local Storage
Gemini / Groq API Keys Call AI APIs on your behalf Firestore (UID-restricted)
AutoForm field data (name, student ID…) Auto-fill forms when you trigger it Local Storage + Firestore

3. What We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not log keystrokes or track what you type on any website.
  • We do not read or transmit browsing history, cookies, or page content unless you explicitly click a fill/AI button.
  • We do not collect analytics or crash reports to any third-party service.
  • We do not share data with advertisers or anyone outside Firebase and the AI APIs you self-configure.

4. How We Use Your Data

  • Authenticate you when logging in.
  • Sync settings (API Keys, AutoForm fields) across your own devices.
  • Forward your API Key to Gemini/Groq only when you trigger an AI request.
  • Deliver in-app update announcements from the developer.

5. Data Storage & Security

  • Chrome Local Storage: Stored only on your browser — no one else can access it.
  • Firebase Firestore: Protected by Security Rules — each user can only access their own document (by UID). Admins can view the full user list for support purposes only.
  • Passwords: Fully managed by Firebase Authentication with industry-standard encryption. We never see your password in plaintext.
API Key Notice: Your API keys are protected by your login credentials. Do not share your account. The developer is not liable for key exposure caused by unauthorised access to your device.

6. Browser Permissions Explained

Permission Reason
activeTab / scripting Inject fill-scripts only when you click a button. Never runs in the background automatically.
storage Save API Keys and AutoForm fields locally in the browser.
clipboardRead Support copy-answer features in certain quiz helpers.
<all_urls> AutoForm must work on any Google Form URL — these cannot be predicted in advance. The script only activates on your click and never monitors arbitrary pages.

7. Children's Privacy

Khoahihi Tools is designed for university students and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

8. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy as the extension evolves. The "Effective" date at the top reflects the latest revision. Continued use constitutes acceptance.

  • Chrome Local Storage: Lưu cục bộ trên trình duyệt của bạn — không ai khác truy cập được.
